Our Story

A little taste of Brazil.

At Costa Café & More we bring the warmth of a Brazilian café to Port St. Lucie. From the first cafezinho of the morning to a loaded açaí bowl in the afternoon, everything is made fresh and served with a smile.

Our cases fill each day with golden pão de queijo, savory salgados, homemade cakes and sweet treats — the perfect partner to a strong Brazilian coffee. Come hungry, leave happy.
