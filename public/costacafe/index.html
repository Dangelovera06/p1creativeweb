Our Story

A little corner of Brazil — built for slow mornings and sweet afternoons.

From the first coffee of the morning to a loaded açaí bowl in the afternoon, everything is made fresh and served with a smile.

Costa Café & More brings the warmth of a Brazilian café to Port St. Lucie. Our cases fill each day with warm breads, savory bites, homemade cakes and sweet treats — the perfect partner to a strong Brazilian coffee. Come hungry, leave happy.
