Questions

FAQ.

Direct answers. If something isn't covered here, ask on the call.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Primarily local service businesses with real margin — med spas, dental practices, aesthetics clinics, contractors, attorneys, and similar operators. The system works best when you have an established offer, real client results, and are ready to scale intentionally.

What's actually included in "done for you"?

Content production (filming, editing, delivery), ad creative and copy, campaign setup and management across Meta platforms, and full AI booking integration with your existing calendar. We handle strategy, execution, and optimization. You approve content and review weekly reports.

How long before we see results?

Most clients see inbound leads within 2–4 weeks of launch. A full pipeline typically builds over 60–90 days as the ad algorithm optimizes and content builds authority. We set realistic expectations on the strategy call based on your market and starting point.

What does the AI booking engine actually do?

When a lead opts in from an ad or form, the AI responds within seconds — personalized to the context. It handles objections, answers questions, and pushes toward a booked appointment. Studies show you're 21× more likely to qualify a lead if you respond within 5 minutes. Our AI does it in seconds, 24/7.

Do I need to be on camera?

Yes — and it's non-negotiable. The businesses that convert best are the ones where the owner or key provider shows their face. Local service businesses win on trust, and trust is built by a person, not a logo. We coach you through it and handle everything else.

What platforms do you run ads on?

Primarily Meta (Facebook and Instagram) — the most efficient platform for local service lead generation at scale. We add TikTok or Google where it makes sense for the specific niche and market. We don't spread thin across every platform; we go deep on what actually converts.

What's your pricing?

We price based on the scope of the build and your market. The strategy call is free and we'll give you a straight number before any commitment. We don't do hourly work or retainers priced on the low end to win the deal — we price to build the system right the first time.

Why one client per niche per city?

Because we build real competitive advantage, not a template. If we're running your ads and developing your brand strategy, we can't ethically do the same for your direct competitor in the same city. First to claim their niche gets the slot.

What if it doesn't work?

We have a clear performance conversation before we start — if the system isn't generating leads by week six, we diagnose together and adjust before billing continues. We don't hide behind vague deliverables. If something isn't working, we say so and fix it.

How is the strategy call free? What's in it?

We review your current content, ad account (if you have one), and follow-up process. You get a written summary of what's working, what's broken, and a recommended priority order to fix it. Takes 30–45 minutes. Costs you nothing. Whether we work together or not, you leave with a real diagnosis.
