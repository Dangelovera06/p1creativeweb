Our Story

A taste of Lima,
rooted in Port St. Lucie.

At Aji Peru, food is memory. Every dish traces back to the open-air markets of Lima, the street cevicherías of Miraflores, and the family kitchens of the Andes — brought to the Treasure Coast unchanged and uncompromised.

From hand-picked ají amarillo to the freshest seafood and slow-braised proteins, every ingredient honors the tradition behind it.

Peruvian Heritage

Five thousand years in every bite.

Peruvian cuisine is five millennia of indigenous tradition, fused with Spanish, Japanese, Chinese, and African influence — one of the world's most celebrated culinary cultures. We don't just cook it. We translate it.

The Ají Pepper

Over 3,000 pepper varieties across every region and tradition in Peru.

Lima's Coastline

Some of the world's richest waters — perfect for ceviche and tiradito.

Chifa Fusion

Family Tradition
